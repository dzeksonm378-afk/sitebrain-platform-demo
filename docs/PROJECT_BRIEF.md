# Project Brief

## Product

SiteBrain is an AI platform for cameras and computer vision. It turns a video stream into operational events, safety violations, logistics state changes, and progress reports.

The product story is simple:

```text
Camera / Video
-> Python AI Worker
-> YOLO / Computer Vision
-> Event
-> SiteBrain Platform
-> Notification / Report
```

## Demo Goal

The SiteBrain Web Platform Demo is a public demo interface that can be opened by QR code and shown to experts during a live presentation.

The expert should quickly understand:

- cameras are connected;
- AI detects meaningful events;
- events appear in the platform;
- a responsible user can confirm, reject, or resolve them;
- the same platform can support safety, logistics, and progress monitoring.

## Demo Directions

### SiteBrain Safety Camera

Detects safety-related events:

- missing helmet;
- missing vest;
- dangerous zone entry;
- safety violations;
- notifications.

### SiteBrain Logistics Camera

Detects warehouse and logistics events:

- pallets arriving and leaving;
- product movement;
- water, juice, and beer tracking;
- occupied/free zone state;
- long stay in a zone.

### SiteBrain Progress Camera

Turns scheduled camera snapshots into progress insights:

- scheduled snapshots;
- timelapse;
- progress tracking;
- activity by zones;
- daily reports.

## Product Positioning

SiteBrain is presented as an AI control center for sites, warehouses, and construction objects. The demo should feel practical, visual, and credible, not theoretical.
